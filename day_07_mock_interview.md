## 🔧 Practice Exercises

### Exercise 1: Technical Mock Interview (40 min)
**Work through these questions aloud or with a partner:**

**Statistics & Probability:**
1. Explain the difference between Type I and Type II errors
2. When would you use a t-test vs a z-test?
3. How does the central limit theorem work?
4. Explain p-value to a non-technical stakeholder
5. What's the difference between correlation and causation?

**Machine Learning:**
1. Explain the bias-variance tradeoff
2. How does regularization work?
3. When would you use random forest vs gradient boosting?
4. Explain how K-means clustering works
5. What's the difference between supervised and unsupervised learning?

**Python/Pandas:**
1. How do you handle missing data in Pandas?
2. What's the difference between merge and join?
3. How would you optimize a slow Pandas operation?
4. Explain groupby in Pandas
5. How do you work with time series data in Pandas?

**SQL:**
1. Write a query to find the second highest salary
2. Explain the difference between WHERE and HAVING
3. How do window functions work?
4. What's the difference between INNER and LEFT JOIN?
5. How would you find duplicate records in a table?

**Data Visualization:**
1. When would you use a box plot vs a histogram?
2. How do you choose the right visualization for your data?
3. What makes a visualization misleading?
4. Explain how you would show trends over time
5. How do you handle overplotting in scatter plots?

### Exercise 2: Product Sense Case Study (30 min)
**Scenario:** A food delivery app wants to increase the number of repeat orders.

**Tasks:**
1. Clarify the goal: what does "increase repeat orders" mean?
2. Understand the users: who are we targeting? New users, inactive users, etc.
3. Brainstorm potential reasons why users don't reorder
4. Propose 3-4 specific product interventions
5. For each intervention, define how you would measure success
6. Prioritize using a framework (RICE, impact/effort, etc.)
7. Design an experiment to test the top intervention
8. What would be your go/no-go criteria?

### Exercise 3: System Design Challenge (25 min)
**Scenario:** Design a real-time fraud detection system for credit card transactions.

**Tasks:**
1. Define the requirements: latency, accuracy, volume
2. Identify the data sources needed
3. Sketch the system architecture (ingestion, processing, storage, decision)
4. Choose appropriate technologies for each component
5. How would you handle model updates and retraining?
6. What metrics would you monitor to ensure the system is working?
7. How would you handle false positives and false negatives?
8. Discuss privacy and security considerations

### Exercise 4: Behavioral Interview Practice (20 min)
**Use the STAR method to prepare answers for:**

1. "Tell me about a time you used data to solve a business problem."
2. "Describe a situation where you had to learn a new technology quickly."
3. "Tell me about a time you disagreed with a teammate and how you resolved it."
4. "Describe a project that didn't go as planned and what you learned."
5. "Give an example of how you've improved a process or workflow."
6. "Tell me about a time you had to explain a complex technical concept to a non-technical audience."

### Exercise 5: Weak Area Identification & Review (15 min)
**Tasks:**
1. Review your notes from Days 1-6
2. Identify 3 topics you feel least confident about
3. For each topic, write down:
   - What specifically you find challenging
   - One resource to review for this topic
   - One practice problem to work on
4. Create a 15-minute review plan for each weak area

### Exercise 6: Interview Day Preparation (10 min)
**Tasks:**
1. List 5 questions you want to ask the interviewer
2. Prepare your "tell me about yourself" story (60-90 seconds)
3. Choose your outfit and prepare your interview environment
4. Plan your day: meals, breaks, mental preparation
5. Write down 3 positive affirmations for interview day
